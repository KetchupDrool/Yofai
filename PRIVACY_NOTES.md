# Privacy Notes — Yofai

Use this text as the basis for a public privacy policy page. Host it at your Privacy Policy URL before App Store review.

**Last updated:** 2026-08-07

## Summary
Yofai is a local-only iPhone photo editing app. It does not require an account and does not use a backend.

## What Yofai Does
- Lets you import a photo from your photo library using the system photo picker
- Lets you rotate and apply simple filters on your device
- Lets you tap **Save Copy** to save an edited copy to Photos
- Keeps a small local history of saves (thumbnail, date, filter, rotation) on your device using on-device storage

## What Yofai Does Not Do
- No account or login
- No backend or cloud sync
- No ads
- No tracking or analytics SDKs
- No selling of personal data
- No AI API calls in this version

## Photos Access
Yofai requests permission to **add** photos only so it can save an edited copy when you tap **Save Copy**.

Yofai does not upload your photos. Edited copies are saved to your Photos library on your device when you choose Save Copy. History entries stored in the app are local app records only; deleting a history row does not delete the photo from Photos.

## Data Stored on Device
- Local edit history records (optional metadata and thumbnails)
- No account profile
- No server-side storage

## Children’s Privacy
Yofai does not collect personal information. There is no account system.
