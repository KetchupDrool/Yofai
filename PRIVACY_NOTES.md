# Privacy Notes — Yofai

Basis for the public privacy policy. Host at the Privacy Policy URL before App Store review.  
App Store Connect questionnaire answers: `APP_STORE_CONNECT_PRIVACY.md`.

**Last updated:** 2026-08-10

## Summary
Yofai is a local-first iPhone app for marketplace product photo preparation and local JPEG export. It does not require an account and does not use a backend for the Free local export workflow.

## What Yofai Does
- Lets you create product projects and organize photo sets on device
- Lets you capture product photos with the camera and import photos via the system picker
- Lets you check, edit, crop, and fit photos for marketplace export sizes
- Lets you export local JPEGs for **manual** upload in marketplace apps/websites
- Keeps export history, optional export notes, and listing drafts on device
- Optionally saves a listing-ready copy to Photos when you choose **Save Listing Copy**

## What Yofai Does Not Do
- No account or login required for local export
- No backend or cloud sync in this version
- No ads
- No tracking or analytics SDKs
- No selling of personal data
- No AI service receives user photos or listing data; Yofai uses deterministic local checks, not AI
- No marketplace upload or publish
- No live marketplace OAuth in this version
- No Yofai Pro purchase in this version (Pro is planned only)

## Photos & Camera
- **Camera:** capture product photos into a local Item Project.
- **Photo library add:** save a listing-ready copy only when you choose Save Listing Copy.
- **System photo picker:** choose existing photos for products/edits.

Yofai does not upload your photos to Yofai servers or marketplaces. Local export files stay in app storage until you share or delete them.

## Data Stored on Device
- Product projects, photos, edit state
- Listing drafts and seller defaults
- Export batch folders, export history metadata, optional notes
- Optional Keychain placeholders for a future Etsy connection (live OAuth not enabled)
- No account profile, no server-side storage

## Children’s Privacy
Yofai does not collect personal information through an account system.
