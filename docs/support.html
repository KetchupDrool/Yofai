Yofai

Yofai is a local-only photo editing app for iPhone. Import a photo you select, rotate it, try Original / Mono / Sepia / Vivid filters, and tap Save Copy when you want an edited version in Photos. History stays on your device.

Email: support@example.com

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No.
  • Are my photos uploaded? No. Editing stays on your device.
  • When does Yofai save to Photos? Only when you tap Save Copy.
  • What is History? A local list of recent saves on your device. Deleting a history item does not delete the photo from Photos.
  • Ads or tracking? None in this version.
