Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No for local export.
  • Does Yofai upload to Etsy or eBay? No. Local Export Mode prepares JPEGs for manual upload.
  • Is Yofai Pro available? Not yet. Free keeps the core local export workflow. No purchase is charged in this version.
  • Where do my photos live? On your device. Export history and notes stay local.
  • Ads or tracking? None in this version.
  • AI features? Not connected in this version.
