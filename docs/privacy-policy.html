Privacy Policy

Yofai · Last updated: August 7, 2026

Summary

Yofai is a local-only iPhone photo editing app. It does not require an account and does not use a backend.

What Yofai does

  • Lets you select a photo from your library using the system photo picker
  • Lets you rotate and apply simple filters on your device
  • Lets you tap Save Copy to save an edited copy to Photos
  • Keeps a small edit history (thumbnail, date, filter, rotation) on your device

What Yofai does not do

  • No account or login
  • No backend or cloud sync
  • No ads
  • No tracking or analytics
  • No selling of personal data

Photos

Photos are selected by you. Yofai requests permission to add photos only so it can save an edited copy when you tap Save Copy.

Yofai does not upload your photos. Edited copies are saved to your Photos library on your device only when you choose Save Copy. History stays on your device. Deleting a history row does not delete the photo from Photos.

Data stored on device

  • Local edit history records and optional thumbnails
  • No account profile
  • No server-side storage

Children’s privacy

Yofai does not collect personal information. There is no account system.
