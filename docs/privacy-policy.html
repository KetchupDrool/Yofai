Privacy Policy

Yofai · Last updated: August 10, 2026

Summary

Yofai is a local-first iPhone app for marketplace product photo preparation and local JPEG export. It does not require an account and does not use a backend for the Free local export workflow.

What Yofai does

  • Lets you create product projects and organize photo sets on your device
  • Lets you capture product photos with the camera and choose photos with the system picker
  • Lets you check, edit, crop, and fit photos for marketplace export sizes
  • Lets you export local JPEGs for manual upload in marketplace apps or websites
  • Keeps export history, optional notes, and listing drafts on your device
  • Optionally saves a listing-ready copy to Photos when you choose Save Listing Copy

What Yofai does not do

  • No account or login required for local export
  • No backend or cloud sync in this version
  • No ads
  • No tracking or analytics SDKs
  • No selling of personal data
  • No AI service — Photo Check and readiness tips are deterministic local tools
  • No marketplace upload or publish
  • No live marketplace OAuth in this version
  • No Yofai Pro purchase in this version

Camera and Photos

Yofai uses the camera to capture product photos into a local Item Project. It may add a photo to your Photos library only when you choose Save Listing Copy. The system photo picker lets you select existing photos.

Yofai does not upload your photos to Yofai servers or to marketplaces. Local export files stay in app storage until you share or delete them.

Data stored on device

  • Product projects, photos, and edit state
  • Listing drafts and seller defaults
  • Export batches, export history metadata, and optional notes
  • No account profile and no server-side storage

Children’s privacy

Yofai does not collect personal information through an account system.
